## 📖 About the Portfolio

This portfolio is designed to reflect my personality as a developer. It is clean, modern, and fully responsive, showcasing my projects, skills, and certificates.

### 🔹 Key Sections:

- **Navbar** – Smooth navigation between Home, About, Projects, Certificates, and Contact
- **About Me** – Brief introduction about my passion and development journey
- **Skills** – A highlight of my tech stack including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Node.js, MongoDB, and Responsive Design
- **Projects & Certificates** – Links to my work and achievements
- **Contact & CV** – Easy way to connect with me and download my resume
